A thoughtful swap is a safer swap.

Check the item’s photos, description, condition, and your swap partner’s profile before agreeing to an exchange.

Meet somewhere public

Arrange handovers in a busy public place during the day. Tell someone you trust where you are going. Keep your home address and exact location private.

Agree on delivery together

If you choose delivery, discuss packaging, shipping costs, and tracking with your partner before sending anything. Inspect the item before confirming completion.

Keep the conversation on Swapify

Use Messages to confirm the item and exchange details. If anything feels wrong, pause the exchange.
