A few things you might be wondering.

Does swapping cost anything?{% lucide "plus" size=20 %}

The pieces on Swapify are offered for exchange, with no purchase price. If you choose delivery, discuss shipping costs with your swap partner and agree on them before sending anything.

What can I list?{% lucide "plus" size=20 %}

Start with clothing you no longer reach for that someone else could enjoy. Add clear photos, the size, and an honest description of its condition, including any flaws. Only list pieces you own and are ready to swap.

How does a swap proposal work?{% lucide "plus" size=20 %}

Open a piece you like and offer one of your available listings in return. Its owner can review your proposal and accept or decline it. Sending a proposal starts the conversation; it doesn’t mean the exchange has happened.

How do meetup and delivery work?{% lucide "plus" size=20 %}

After acceptance, agree on a public meetup or arrange delivery together in Messages. Confirm the place and time, or the packaging, shipping costs, and tracking details. Swapify doesn’t book a courier for you. Read our safety guidance before making a plan.

What happens after acceptance?{% lucide "plus" size=20 %}

A chat and an exchange record help you arrange the next steps. Check the piece when you receive it, then confirm the exchange through your swap details. After completion, you can leave a review to help the next person get to know your swap partner.
