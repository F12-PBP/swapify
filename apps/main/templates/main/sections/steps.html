Four steps to a brand new favourite.

Swapping is simpler than you think. Here's how a Swapify trade works from first post to happy unboxing.

  1. 1

    List Your Item

    Snap a few photos, write a quick description, and post your item to the Swapify marketplace in under 3 minutes.

  2. 2

    Browse & Match

    Scroll through thousands of listings. When something catches your eye, send a swap proposal to its owner.

  3. 3

    Agree & Arrange

    Chat in-app, confirm the swap terms, and choose how to exchange — local meetup or shipping.

  4. 4

    Swap & Enjoy

    Both parties confirm receipt, leave reviews, and repeat. Your wardrobe stays fresh without the spend.
